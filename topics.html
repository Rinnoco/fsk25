XXXIX Panhellenic Conference on Solid State Physics & Materials Science, 14-17 September 2025, Paphos

Scope & topics

The conference serves as a dynamic platform for exploring the latest advancements and breakthroughs in the fields of Condensed Matter and Materials Science. Experts, researchers, and industry stakeholders from diverse backgrounds converge to share knowledge and foster collaborations. Discover the scope and topics that define our gathering, each designed to inspire, challenge, and enrich our understanding of the world around us. Each of the topics below welcomes both experimental and theoretical/computational works.


Photonics and Optoelectronics
- Nanophotonics, Plasmonics & Metamaterials
- Materials, Devices, and Applications
- Nonlinear & active optical materials
- Quantum Photonics

Micro/Nano Electronics

Low Dimensional Materials

Materials for Energy
