Accommodation

We are pleased to inform you of our special arrangements for your accommodation during the 38th Panhellenic Conference on Solid State Physics and Materials Science at the Deluxe GRAND SERAI CONGRESS & SPA HOTEL, located conveniently in the heart of Ioannina city.

Spanning 13 acres, the GRAND SERAI offers a unique concept of hospitality, ensuring a memorable experience. The hotel features 216 beautifully decorated rooms, traditional hayati, and luxury suites, all equipped with high-standard amenities to satisfy even the most demanding guests. Additionally, the GRAND SERAI SPA invites you to indulge in a world of care, pampering, and relaxation.

While we recommend this esteemed hotel for its comfort and convenience, Ioannina city also offers a wide range of alternative accommodation options should you prefer to make other arrangements.

We look forward to welcoming you and ensuring a pleasant stay in Ioannina.

Should you wish to make your reservations with Grand Serai, please send an e-mail to info@grandserai.com to Mrs. Eleni Petrelli (reservations manager), mentioning "fsk2024" to ensure the special offer for the conference (shown below)
