Committed to preserving the city’s rich cultural heritage while fostering sustainable growth, the municipality supports a wide range of initiatives that enhance public life, promote community engagement, and strengthen the city’s identity both locally and internationally.


Rinnoco LTD is a research, innovation, and education consulting company, which enables the development of new unique products from design to deployment targeting international markets.
Founded in 2021, Rinnoco is a startup company based in Limassol, Cyprus, with unique expertise due to its broad experienced team in both academic and industry. The founders of the company have over 30 years of diverse research experience, including SQL and NoSQL databases, big data and data streams, spatiotemporal systems, mobile computing, sensor networks and internet of things (IoT), machine learning applications, quantum mechanics, and significant scientific contributions published in international top-tier conferences and scientific journals.
Rinnoco LTD’s vision is to become the first of the kind company in Cyprus, which develops state-of-the-art data management techniques and provides novel data management tools that can boost the productivity, profitability, and scalability of local and international companies.

Hellenic Society for the Science and Technology of Condensed Matter (HSSTCM) is a Greek non-profit scientific society founded in 1981. HSSTCM is a member of FEMS (Federation of European Materials Societies), participates in their general assemblies and has the right of electing and being elected at the Administrative Board.


Coherent Corp. is a global leader in materials, networking, and lasers for the industrial, communications, electronics, and instrumentation markets. The company is headquartered in Saxonburg, Pennsylvania. It was founded in 1971 to manufacture high-quality materials and optics for industrial lasers. Today, the company operates in more than 20 countries around the world.
Coherent is focused on delivering innovations that fuel market megatrends while pursuing our mission of enabling the world to be safer, healthier, closer, and more efficient. Coherent empowers market innovators to define the future through breakthrough technologies, from materials to systems.

Lumoscribe is a Photonics-based company specializing in developing sensors and lasers. Specifically, Lumoscribe uses the technology of Optical Fibers, to produce high-quality sensors, lasers, and complete optical sensing solutions for various applications.
As an SME company, whose entire business model is based on advanced technology, Lumoscribe defines itself as a deep-tech company; a company that generates, directs, and manipulates light to develop and commercialize high-tech engineering innovations and applicable scientific advances.
Furthermore, through its own Photonics Research Center where new technologies are developed and applied, Lumoscribe supports organizations to prototype and develop their own solutions.

Vector Technologies, which is certified with the international standard of ISO 9001:2015, has a significant and in depth experience and offers innovative solutions and measuring systems on the market of telecommunications and electronics.
With new organizational structure and constantly oriented in technological innovations, we can achieve even greater efficiency for the services provided and help our customers in choosing the best solutions corresponding to their needs.
Our mission is to provide to our customers the most suitable solutions for their specific requirements, always combining the highest quality at the most affordable prices.
Our strategy is working in cooperation with the world's leading suppliers, along with the high expertise of our people and this is a guarantee of quality and success of our services to every electronic application.
Our facilities are located at Vrilissia Attikis and besides the offices include warehouses, integration center, demo center and training facilities.

SPECS leads the way in state-of-the-art technology, cutting-edge components and individually designed complex systems for surface analysis. Rigaku Corporation, has been at the forefront of analytical and industrial instrumentation technology, since its inception in Japan in 1951. A world leader in the fields of WD-, ED- and TXRF- X-Ray Fluorescence Spectrometry, X-Ray Diffraction, SAXS Systems, X-Ray Rotating Anode Generators and Goniometers. Rigaku is also leader in Small Molecule and Protein Crystallography, Special X-Ray Optics, Differential Scanning Calorimeters (DSC) and combined XRD+DSC systems, X-Ray Industrial Radiography as well as Vacuum, Cryogenics, EVA, Stress, VPD Systems etc. More details: https://rigaku.com/
